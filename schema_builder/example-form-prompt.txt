Create a recipe for a JSON form titled "Project Protege Mentor Registration".

This form consists of 7 pages, (not including the declaration page), structured in the following order, with the labels for each of their nested fields:

1. Applicant Information:
  - First Name
  - Last Name
  - Date of Birth
  - Employment Status (text field)
  - Institution Name
  - Employer Name
  - Other Employment Details (text field)
2. Contact Information
  - Address Line 1
  - Address Line 2
  - Parish 
  - Email Address 
  - Telephone Number
3. Mentorship Information
  - Why do you want to be a mentor (textarea)
  - What are your strenghts that would benefit a mentee? (textarea)
  - What would you like your mentee to learn from you (textarea)
4. Preferences
  - Mentee Gender Preference (Select with options: male, female, either)
  - Are you willing to share your phone number with your mentee? (radio: yes, no)
  - Do you have a specific mentee in mind? (Radio: yes, no)
  - Name of mentee you have in mind (Only if "do you have a specific mentee in mind" is yes)
  - Mentee Phone number (Only if do you have a specifc mentee in mind is yes.)
5. Mentoring Experience
  - Do you have previous mentoring experience? (Radio: yes, no)
  - Years of mentoring experience (number, maxValue: 50. Only if do you have previous mentoring experience is 'yes')
6. Professional Reference
  - First Name 
  - Last Name
  - Relationship 
  - Email 
  - Phone number
7. Personal Reference
  - First Name
  - Last Name 
  - Relationship 
  - Email 
  - Phone 

Note: The Recipe should be written as a JSON file.
